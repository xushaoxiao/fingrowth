# Risk and privacy boundaries

This is an educational planning framework. Local lending, privacy, advertising, fair-treatment and consumer-protection requirements must be evaluated by qualified owners in each jurisdiction. It is not a compliance certification or legal advice.

No real customer-level data, protected-attribute inference, secrets, production endpoints or employer materials belong in this repository. Anonymous aggregates can still disclose small populations; use approved suppression and disclosure controls outside this synthetic demo. Separate growth optimization from underwriting authority. Stop or roll back on predefined fraud, loss, complaint or consent breaches; thresholds must come from authorized policy, never generic benchmark invention. Record approvals and delayed outcome review. Security scans reduce risk but cannot prove absence of sensitive information.
