## Assessment of the colleague's proposal

1. **Keep all assigned eligible people in the primary denominator.** Upload is affected by the intervention. Restricting to uploaders selects a post-treatment population, can destroy comparability and would hide people helped or deterred from uploading. Upload-conditioned rates can be labeled exploratory diagnostics; they cannot replace the assigned-person primary metric.
2. **Do not test p < 0.05 daily under a fixed-horizon plan.** Repeated opportunities to declare success increase false-positive risk. Inspect safety and data quality according to approved rules, and wait for the prespecified primary analysis. If valid sequential monitoring was planned before exposure, use its exact rules. If efficacy peeking or outcome-driven changes already occurred, disclose them and obtain statistical review; treat the pilot as exploratory and plan a fresh confirmatory test where needed. A new rule cannot retroactively validate a stopping decision.
3. **Zero recorded losses before any loan is due is an immature observation.** It is not evidence of zero eventual credit loss, safe risk-adjusted growth or positive mature contribution. Preserve existing underwriting and pricing; obtain due-date, outstanding exposure and maturity information and wait for the approved risk/finance observation horizon.
